The Story

Built for women who want to feel refreshed, not recognised

Jamelia Aesthetics began with a quiet frustration. Too many clients were arriving at our door after treatments elsewhere that felt rushed, overdone, or — worse — unsafe. They didn’t want a new face. They wanted their own face, looking well.

So we built the opposite of a clinic conveyor belt. A small, cream-toned sanctuary in Wuse 2 where appointments are longer than they need to be, where you are asked about your skin, your diary, your hormones and your expectations before a needle ever comes out — and where “no” is a perfectly acceptable answer.

Our practitioners train to UK clinical standards and treat every treatment as a medical decision, not a transaction. We stock only regulated, traceable products. We document everything. We follow up. And we will always tell you when the most beautiful option is the smallest one.

What We Stand For

Four commitments, every visit

These aren’t marketing lines — they are the rules we work to, whether you’re here for a Hydra Facial or a full facial harmonisation.

Safety before beauty

Regulated products, sterile technique, full medical history and an honest suitability assessment. If a treatment isn’t right for you, we’ll say so.

Subtle by design

We dose conservatively and build gradually. The goal is a rested, healthy version of your own face — not a template.

Education, not pressure

You’ll leave every consultation understanding the product, the risks, the downtime and the cost — before you decide anything.

Aftercare is the treatment

Follow-up messages, lymphatic support, faja fitting and private recovery accommodation — because results are made in the weeks after.

The Jamelia Standard

Clinical rigour, spa-level calm

Aesthetic medicine is medicine. Behind the soft lighting and warm towels sits a strict clinical process — the same one you’d expect from a reputable UK practice.

  • Comprehensive medical history and contraindication screening
  • Only regulated, batch-traceable products from reputable suppliers
  • Patch testing, aspiration and cannula-first technique where appropriate
  • Written aftercare, emergency contact and scheduled review appointments
  • Complete discretion — private suites, no shared waiting areas

Before You Visit

Questions, answered

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes — for all injectables, threads, skin boosters and body contouring, a consultation is required before treatment. It lets us review your medical history, assess suitability and give you written information and pricing to consider at home.

Will my results look obvious?

Our entire approach is built around subtlety. We typically start conservatively and build over time, and we’ll tell you honestly if we think a smaller dose — or no treatment at all — will serve you better.

What products do you use?

Only regulated, batch-traceable products sourced from reputable suppliers. You are always welcome to ask which product will be used, view the packaging and check its batch number before your treatment begins.

Where exactly are you based?

We’re in Wuse 2, Abuja, FCT — open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM. Exact directions and parking details are sent when your appointment is confirmed, or you can message us on Instagram at @jamelia_aesthetics.

Is my appointment private?

Completely. Treatments take place in a private suite, appointments are staggered so you won’t meet another client, and we never share images or details without your explicit written consent.
